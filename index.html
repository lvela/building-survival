Building Survival

Your flight is going down over the city. When it slams into a tower, you wake up on the 10th floor with fire everywhere. Get down all ten floors and out the lobby doors before the building collapses.

VR needs a WebXR headset browser, like the Meta Quest Browser.

Screen controls

  • WASD move · mouse to look
  • Shift sprint · Space jump
  • C crouch · Enter skip intro

VR controls

  • Left stick move · right stick turn
  • Grip sprint · A/X or trigger jump
  • Crouch for real under low beams

Hazards

  • Fire burns. Go around it.
  • Live wires: cross when they go dark
  • Red ring: ceiling about to fall
  • Jump rubble, crouch under beams

Objective

  • Each stairwell is blocked below you
  • Cross the floor to the green EXIT stairs
  • 10 floors · 3:30 on the clock
